I am an Assistant Professor at the [Department of Industrial Engineering](https://tec.mx/en/innovation-and-transformation/bs-in-industrial-engineering) at [Tecnologico de Monterrey](https://tec.mx/en). My main research area is the statistical design and analysis of experiments to develop and improve complex processes and products. I am the Program Chair-Elect (2027-2028) of the [Quality and Productivity Section](https://community.amstat.org/qp/home) of the [American Statistical Association](https://www.amstat.org/), and a member of the Editorial board of [Quality Engineering](https://www.tandfonline.com/journals/lqen20) and [Journal of Quality Technology](https://www.tandfonline.com/journals/ujqt20). I am also part of the [Sistema Nacional de Investigadoras e Investigadores](https://conahcyt.mx/sistema-nacional-de-investigadores/), Level 1.


My academic experience includes:

- Council member of the [Quality, Statistics, and Reliability section](https://connect.informs.org/qsr/home) of [INFORMS](https://www.informs.org/) (2023 to 2026). 

- Assistant Professor at the [Department of Industrial Engineering](https://industrial-engineering.uark.edu/) at the [University of Arkansas](https://www.uark.edu/) (2022 to 2023). 

- Assistant Adjunct Professor at the [Department of Statistics](http://statistics.ucla.edu/) at the [University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA)](https://ucla.edu/) (2020 to 2022). 

- Junior Postdoctoral Fellow of the Research Foundation Flanders ([FWO](https://www.fwo.be/en/)) and [KU Leuven](https://www.biw.kuleuven.be/biosyst/english), Belgium (2018 to 2020). 

In May 2018, I obtained a Ph.D. in Applied Economics at the [University of Antwerp](https://www.uantwerpen.be/en/about-uantwerp/faculties/faculty-of-business-and-economics/), Belgium. The supervisors of my Ph.D. thesis were Prof. [Peter Goos](http://www.experimental-design.eu/who/) and Dr. [Eric Schoen](http://www.experimental-design.eu/who/). I was born and raised in Monterrey, Nuevo Leon, Mexico.
